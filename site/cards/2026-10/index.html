Set 4: The Rebuy

Eleven cards, minted from the October 13 game. Real players, real stats: every number on every card comes from the two official logs, 204 hands across two tables. Rarity is earned, not assigned. Winning the night takes the foil.

The set is named for the champion's second bullet. Matt W. was out of chips at 7:27, bought back in, and held the title at 9:15. Two players took their first cards tonight, and one of them, back at the table after a break, left with a skull.
