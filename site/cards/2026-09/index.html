Set 3: Seven-Deuce

Fourteen cards, minted from the September 8 game. Real players, real stats: every number on every card comes from the two official logs, 338 hands across two tables. Rarity is earned, not assigned. Winning the night takes the foil.

The set is named for the worst hand in poker. Seven-deuce was shown down eleven times on the night, beat pocket aces once, and was outdone only by the three-deuce that won the title on the final hand.

One card breaks the placement ladder on purpose. The Hope Coin sets a floor: whoever holds it never falls below rare, whatever the night did to them.
