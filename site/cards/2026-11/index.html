Set 5: Bangkok

Twelve cards, minted from the November 10 game. Real players, real stats: every number on every card comes from the two official logs, 236 hands across two tables. Rarity is earned, not assigned. Winning the night takes the foil.

The set is named for where the house played it from. For Mike M. the cards went in the air at ten in the morning, Bangkok time, and the game went out under the poster above. Josh B. took the foil with the last five knockouts and a jack-high straight flush. One player took his first card tonight and left with a skull.
