Set 2: Wire to Wire

Seven cards, minted from the August 11 game. Real players, real stats: every number on every card comes from the 211-hand tournament record. Rarity is earned, not assigned. Winning the night takes the foil.

The set is named for how it was won. Thomas D. led 143 of the 211 hands, knocked out four of the six players who busted, and never gave the table a turn at the front.

One card breaks the placement ladder on purpose. The Hope Coin sets a floor: whoever holds it never falls below rare, whatever the night did to them.
