How to play

  • Each turn, pull one tooth and choose its kind: incisor, canine, premolar or molar (8 at most). Every tooth you have pulled is usable again each turn.
  • Teeth pay for cards. A card's cost shows the teeth it insists on, then a number for teeth of any kind. Gold teeth count as any kind.
  • Click a card in your hand to play it. Dentists take a chair; procedures happen once and are gone.
  • A dentist can drill starting the turn after it arrives. Click it, then click an enemy dentist or the other patient.
  • Drill is how much it hurts. Nerve is how much a dentist can take before quitting. Front Desk dentists must be dealt with first.
  • Take the other patient from 20 teeth to none.
