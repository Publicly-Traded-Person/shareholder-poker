Standings and trophies

How the night runs

  • When. Second Tuesday of the month, 7:00pm PT. Most nights run about three hours.
  • Where. Cards on Poker Now, faces on the official Zoom. Both, not either.
  • Stakes. $50 buy-in, up to two rebuys before the 8:05pm cutoff. Tournament format, everyone starts on the same stack.
  • Payouts. A pool over $500 pays three places, otherwise it pays two.
  • The Zoom rule. Players on the call may openly collude against players who are not. A dropped connection is forgiven. Sitting out the video all night is a choice, and the table is allowed to notice.
